How do I choose the right area to stay in Bali?

By Anastasiia Nikolaeva · Published 2026-11-24 · Last updated 2026-11-24

Choose one or two areas, not all of them. Bali's areas are very different, and moving between them can take half a day. Go to Ubud for yoga, nature and a calmer pace, Canggu for a busy social scene and surf lessons, or Uluwatu for beaches, sunsets and experienced surfing.

I wrote this from my own time in Bali during my trip through Indonesia, from March 16 to May 14, 2026. Here is how I would think about each area.

Quick comparison

AreaBest forDownside
UbudYoga, meditation, spa, temples, waterfalls, rice fieldsFewer events, less social
CangguParties, groups by interest, learning to surfHeavy traffic, very crowded
UluwatuExperienced surfers, swimming beaches, sunsetsMore expensive, feels separate from the main area

Ubud: spiritual, green and quieter

Ubud is the most spiritual area. It suits people who like yoga, meditation and spa experiences. You can try cacao ceremonies and purification rituals, visit Hindu temples, and enjoy beautiful nature like waterfalls, rice fields and jungles.

It is also closer to quieter areas such as Sidemen, where you can see a volcano and go hiking, if you want a more authentic experience. The downside is that there are far fewer events than in Canggu, and the area is less social.

Canggu: busy, social and full of things to do

Canggu has something for almost every interest. There are many parties and groups for Hyrox, running, dancing, volleyball, blogging, acting and arts. It is also a good place to learn to surf.

The downside is that it is very busy. Traffic is always stuck, and the area feels overcrowded.

Uluwatu: beaches, surf and sunsets, but more expensive

Uluwatu is more expensive, and it is a paradise for experienced surfers. It also has the best beaches for swimming, with clear turquoise water. Check the tides before you go.

You can also find beautiful jungles, amazing sunsets, and several traditional temples where you can watch traditional dances. It felt a little separate from the main area, because it is on a peninsula.

Quieter areas for a more local side of Bali

Further away there are areas like Pemuteran, Lovina, Amed and Sidemen. I would suggest them to travelers who have already seen the popular places and want to see a more local side of Balinese culture and life.

How much time to spend in each area

If you have only one or two weeks, spend quality time in one or two places. Trying to cover everything means a lot of time in transport. Going from Uluwatu to Ubud, for example, can take half a day.

Frequently asked questions

Can I see all of Bali in one or two weeks?

It is possible, but I would not try. With one or two weeks, I would spend quality time in one or two places, because moving between areas can take half a day. Getting from Uluwatu to Ubud is one example.

Which area in Bali is best for surfing?

Canggu is a good place to learn to surf. Uluwatu is a paradise for experienced surfers.

Where in Bali is best for meeting people?

Canggu. There are many parties and groups by interest, such as Hyrox, running, dancing, volleyball, blogging, acting and arts. Ubud is quieter and less social.

This guide is based on my own trip, and things in Bali can change. Check current local information before you go.
