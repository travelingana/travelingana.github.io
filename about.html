About Anastasiia Nikolaeva

I'm Anastasiia, a solo traveler who spent just over seven months in Asia between December 2025 and July 2026. I write about what I actually did, spent and learned along the way.

My experience

I traveled alone and stayed in all kinds of places: rented apartments, hotels, guest houses and bunk beds in hostels. My route was:

  • China: December 4 to 12, 2025
  • Hong Kong: December 12 to 15, 2025
  • Vietnam: December 15, 2025 to January 22, 2026
  • Thailand: January 22 to March 16, 2026
  • Indonesia: March 16 to May 14, 2026
  • The Philippines: May 14 to July 6, 2026

Why this site

I went to explore new countries, cuisines and cultures, to meet people from all over the world, and to figure out what I want. The Unplanned Route is where I share the practical side of that trip: the questions I had, and the answers I found.
